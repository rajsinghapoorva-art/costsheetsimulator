Cost vocabulary

Choose the cost object first. The same cost can be classified in several valid ways.
TermMeaningExample
Cost objectAnything for which a separate cost is measuredProduct, department, order
Cost unitStandard quantity in which cost is expressedPacket, passenger-km
Cost centreLocation/person/equipment where costs are collectedMixing department, oven
Cost driverFactor that causes a cost to changeMachine hours, set-ups
Classify the same cost by
Nature: material · labour · expense
Function: production · administration · selling / distribution
Traceability: direct · indirect (overhead)
Behaviour: variable · fixed · semi-variable · step-fixed
Direct vs indirect depends on the cost object. A supervisor can be direct to a department and indirect to a biscuit packet.

Economic order quantity

EOQ balances annual ordering cost and carrying cost. Use annual carrying cost per unit (C), not the total annual holding cost.
C = unit price × annual carrying rate. EOQ = √(2AO ÷ C). Model assumes even known demand, constant price/cost/lead time, instant replenishment and no stock-outs. Quantity discounts or seasonal usage need separate comparison.
